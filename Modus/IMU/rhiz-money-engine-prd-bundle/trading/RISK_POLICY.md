# Trading Risk Policy

This is not financial advice. This module is a risk-control scaffold.

## Prime directive

Do not trade money needed for food, rent, bills, tax, or debt.

## Buckets

- Survival: food, rent, bills. Never traded.
- Stability: emergency reserve. Never traded.
- Build Capital: tools, hosting, domains, ads. Invested in business first.
- Speculative: only money that can go to zero.

## Passive crypto approach

- DCA only after survival and reserve are covered.
- Blue-chip-only watchlist if used.
- No yield protocol without smart-contract/custody/liquidity risk review.
- No leverage.

## Aggressive trading approach

Only inside a capped sandbox:

- max 1% of speculative bucket per trade
- max 3 trades/day
- mandatory invalidation
- mandatory journal
- no revenge trades
- 24h cooldown after 2 losses
- no leverage until 90 days profitable on paper
- paper trade first

## Kill switch

Stop trading if hungry, sleep deprived, emotionally escalated, using borrowed money, or breaking journal discipline.
