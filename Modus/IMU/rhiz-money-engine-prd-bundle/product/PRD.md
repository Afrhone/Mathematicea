# PRD — RHIZ Entwine Money Engine

## Objective

Create a monetizable product suite that converts RHIZ/Spectra/Functor infrastructure into paid services, subscriptions, digital products, and controlled trading research.

## Problem

The current project surface is powerful but too broad. It needs a money engine that:

- produces immediate cashflow,
- packages expertise into repeatable offers,
- creates subscription revenue,
- protects against destructive overbuilding,
- separates trading research from survival money,
- turns symbolic/intellectual material into usable products.

## MVP promise

> A dashboard and service workflow that diagnoses and stabilizes AI infrastructure while generating reusable case studies, paid reports, and subscription follow-up.

## MVP modules

1. Ops Doctor
2. Client Report Generator
3. Subscription Guardian
4. Knowledge Cookbook
5. Trading Research Lab

## Out of scope for MVP

- autonomous live trading
- token issuance
- DAO treasury
- high-leverage crypto strategy
- guaranteed passive income claims

## Success metrics

- first CHF 300 paid diagnostic/fix
- 3 paying maintenance clients
- 10 published technical recipes
- 1 paid bundle
- monthly recurring revenue over CHF 1000
- zero trades outside risk policy
