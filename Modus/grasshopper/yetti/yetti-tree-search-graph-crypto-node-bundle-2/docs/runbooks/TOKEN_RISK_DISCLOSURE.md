# YETTI Token Risk Disclosure Draft

YETTI Snow Credits is experimental software.

## No value promise

YETTI has no promised monetary value, no guaranteed market, no redemption right, no profit expectation, and no claim on revenue, assets, equity, dividends, interest, or governance outside the experimental system unless a future reviewed release explicitly changes this.

## Loss risk

A holder may lose access to YETTI in part or in full due to bugs, key loss, chain reset, contract migration, discontinued development, governance failure, or infrastructure failure.

## Transferability and liquidity

YETTI may not always be transferable. YETTI may not be liquid. No market or exchange listing is promised.

## Utility uncertainty

If YETTI is presented as a utility or coordination token, the promised utility may fail, change, or be discontinued.

## Security risk

Smart contracts and node software can contain critical bugs. Audit reports reduce risk but do not remove it.

## Regulatory risk

Authorities may classify or regulate the token differently depending on facts, jurisdiction, marketing, transferability, holder rights, and economic reality.

## Experimental AI / automation risk

The tree-search graph proposes actions; it does not guarantee correctness. Automated actions must remain gated and reviewable.
