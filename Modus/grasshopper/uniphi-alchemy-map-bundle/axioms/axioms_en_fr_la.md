# Uniphi Axioms (Latin • French • English)

These are **motto-style axioms**: short phrases meant to guide design, art, engineering, and self-regulation.
Some lines are traditional; others are **neo-Latin** crafted for modern practice.

## Core Transmutation

1. **Solve et Coagula** — *Dissoudre et coaguler* — Dissolve and coagulate.  
   Break it down; make it real.

2. **Ordo ab Chao** — *L’ordre du chaos* — Order from chaos.  
   Let noise speak, then give it a spine.

3. **Iterare est Distillare** — *Itérer, c’est distiller* — To iterate is to distill.  
   Each pass removes mud and reveals signal.

## Structure, Truth, and Naming

4. **Veritas in Structura** — *La vérité dans la structure* — Truth lives in structure.  
   Clarity is compassion.

5. **Nomen est Clavis** — *Le nom est clé* — Name is a key.  
   Good names unlock good systems.

6. **Mensura est Magia** — *La mesure est magie* — Measurement is magic.  
   What you can observe, you can shape.

## Connection and Boundaries

7. **Nexus supra Murum** — *Le lien au-dessus du mur* — Connection over walls.  
   Build bridges before bunkers.

8. **Custodia sine Carcere** — *Garder sans enfermer* — Guard without imprisoning.  
   Security is a garden fence, not a cage.

9. **Aperire, Non Claudere** — *Ouvrir, pas fermer* — Open, not shut.  
   Design for entry and learning.

## Play, Care, Witness

10. **Ludus est Labor** — *Le jeu est travail* — Play is work.  
    Play is how systems evolve.

11. **Caritas in Codice** — *La bonté dans le code* — Care in the code.  
    Make the humane choice the default choice.

12. **Testis facit Mundum** — *Le témoin fait le monde* — The witness makes the world.  
    Art exists when it can be met.
