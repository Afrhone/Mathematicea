# 90-Day Elliptical Plan

Elliptical planning cycles between cash, build, proof, and distribution.

## Orbit 1 — Survival / Cash
Weekly: sell diagnostic, close one small fix, publish one proof artifact.

## Orbit 2 — Product
Weekly: convert one repeated fix into a product feature.

## Orbit 3 — Media
Weekly: one article, one WebGL demo, one screenshot thread.

## Orbit 4 — Subscription
Monthly: premium cookbook, dashboard update, member invite.

## Orbit 5 — Trading Research
Weekly: journal only. No live trading until cashflow floor exists.

## Milestones

- Day 30: first cash, product, recurring offer.
- Day 60: dashboard MVP, 3 reports, 10 cookbook entries.
- Day 90: CHF 1000+ MRR potential, first workshop, case study.
