# Positioning

## One-line

AI infrastructure rescue and creative systems lab for people building beyond templates.

I help artists, devs, studios, and small labs repair and operate local AI infrastructure: Docker, LXD, OpenWebUI, Ollama, llama.cpp, GPU routing, reverse proxies, and RAG/cookbook systems.

- guaranteed trading profits
- metaphysics as product
- autonomous superintelligence

## Lead with

- broken stack fixed
- saved time
- reproducible scripts
- clear reports
- beautiful interface
- human-readable architecture
