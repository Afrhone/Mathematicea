## Anthropology: inclusive fitness → social behavior

The DAO/social behavior model is represented as:

```text
fitness_contribution = direct_contribution + weighted_peer_contribution
```

This is not biology literalism. It is a governance heuristic:

- reward collaborators who increase ecosystem health
- score proposals by benefit to shared graph
- penalize unsafe entropy and opaque financial flow
- require peer review for mutation of high-trust nodes
